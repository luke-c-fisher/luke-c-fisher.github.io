My work
=======

For my capstone project, I developed and compared two classifiers in R, one logistic regression and the other gradient boosting (xgboost), to predict diabetes on a CDC dataset of 70,000 respondents. I evaluated the models on accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, and ROC-AUC to determine which one was a better fit to predict diabetes. Both models performed similarly, with both exhibiting around 75% accuracy and an AUC of 0.83. However, the gradient boosting model led in sensitivity (80% vs. the logistic model's 77%). I chose it for that reason, as the consequences of missing a true positive case are costlier than a false alarm. This project taught me that, when deciding between two models, the right metric depends on the cost of a wrong prediction.
